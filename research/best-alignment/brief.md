# Research Brief: Best Preference Alignment Method (2024-2026)

**Refined question**: Among all preference alignment / RLHF methods for LLMs (DPO, PPO, GRPO, SPO, SimPO, KTO, ORPO, RLOO, PRM-based, self-rewarding, etc.), which ones are considered "state of the art" as of mid-2026? What are their relative strengths and which production models use which?

**Scope**:
- In: Open-source methods, production deployment evidence (which methods power top models), benchmark results (Chatbot Arena, AlpacaEval, GSM8K, MATH), papers from 2024-2026
- Out: Proprietary-only methods with no public description, methods requiring unreleased infrastructure

**Assumptions**:
- "Best" depends on constraints (compute budget, data availability, model size, task). We need a nuanced comparison, not a single winner.
- The audience is a practitioner who has studied DPO/PPO/GRPO/SPO and wants to know what to learn next.

**Depth**: standard
**Today**: 2026-07-09
